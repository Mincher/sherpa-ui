- **B:** whatever the bar shows is the order, and the bar reports it to the source (saved filters too). The panel copies the bar.

**D3 (348): a chip that is switched OFF.**
- **A (pick):** it does not narrow the chips after it. Its own badge still counts what it would match within the filters before it. This keeps TODO 123.

- **A (pick):** yes. Counts, greying, setting aside and serial sets all go by position. A later Region pick no longer sets an earlier Customer pick aside, so the TODO 180 rule goes. **D5 (349): does "Limit to matching filters" stay an opt-in?**
