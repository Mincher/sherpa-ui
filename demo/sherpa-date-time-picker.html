Sherpa Date-Time Picker

Three trigger inputs that spawn the picker panel in the relevant variant. The picker is positioned with CSS anchor positioning against its trigger.

Date input

Click the field to open a calendar-only picker (data-mode="date").

Time input

Click the field to open a clock-only picker (data-mode="time").

Date & time input

Click the field to open the combined picker (data-mode="date-time", the default).
